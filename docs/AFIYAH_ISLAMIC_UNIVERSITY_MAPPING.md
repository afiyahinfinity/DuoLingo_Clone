The source delivery packs use a 10-question assessment with an 8/10 pass mark. The Afiyah Eight product layer raises the completion gate to **9/10 (90%)**. Source teaching content, terminology, boundaries, and quiz meaning should be preserved unless separately reviewed.

## Source-pack structure we can reuse

Each reviewed pack is already organized for three delivery modes:

1. Trainer lesson plan
2. Participant workbook
3. Self-paced online lesson and quiz

Common source features include:

- learning outcomes
- Arabic / Islamic terminology
- Qur’anic study anchors
- worked fictional cases
- worksheets and actions
- a 10-question final quiz
- answer key and automated feedback
- LMS build notes
- completion / retake rules
- privacy, safeguarding, referral, and governance boundaries

## Afiyah adaptation rules

- Preserve source framing and terminology; do not silently turn educational content into fatwa, legal advice, financial advice, therapy, or diagnosis.
- Use fictional cases in learner assessments where the source pack requires them.
- Store learner progress, attempts, scores, completion state, streaks, and product rewards; do not infer religiosity, poverty, health status, abuse, or other sensitive traits from learning behaviour.
- Module passes at 9/10 or higher in Afiyah.
- Passed Gift Module reward: +8 Infinity product points.
- Arabic learning remains a separate language track using the Duolingo-style lesson engine. A passed Arabic lesson at >=90% earns +16 Infinity +16 Amanah product progression points (2x track). These are product rewards, not claims of religious merit.
- Noor / AnythingLLM should answer only from approved, module-scoped source material for the active module and return citations where supported.
